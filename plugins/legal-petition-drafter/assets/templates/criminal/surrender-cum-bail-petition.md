# Surrender-cum-Bail Petition | Approved reusable layout

**Status:** User-approved format, 8 October 2026. Use this as the default for a request for a surrender-cum-bail petition. Case-specific facts must always come from the new source papers, never from an earlier petition.

## Layout rules
- A4 portrait, English Times New Roman 12 pt; Tinos is the approved matching fallback. Do not silently change typeface.
- **Four-to-five-finger blank gap at the top of EACH page** for this particular template, overriding the repository's general top-gap default; approximate with 65–75 mm, adjust only as needed to preserve legibility. If the new request specifies another gap, follow it.
- Traditional court style, clean black type. Preserve court heading, case number, P.S. case number, sections, and party title as in the supplied papers.
- Indent the text block beginning **“The humble Surrender Cum Bail Petition filed on behalf of…”** distinctly to the **right side** like the approved handwritten reference, not left aligned or centered. Retain coherent line breaks.
- Number the factual grounds and use conventional formal wording; do not invent procedural history or merits. End with the customary indented prayer, with any concluding phrase back at the left margin.
- **No separate certificate page, no certificate, and no Advocate signature line at the foot of the petition**, unless expressly asked in the new request.
- Include **ALL petitioners**, including multiple petitioners, in the case heading, opening, references, and relief, and verify spellings individually.
- For the approved example, the users corrected both petitioners (Pritam Kumar Mishra and Savitri Kumari), G.R. Case No. 182 of 2026, Bagbera P.S. Case No. 52 of 2025. **These are example-only verification notes; NEVER copy them into a different matter.**
- The first-time bail application statement was essential to the approved example. For future cases, ask or confirm prior bail history; only say none has ever been moved before this or any other court when that is independently supplied/verified. Never hard-code this declaration for a new case.
- Aim at a single-page PDF **when the content fits** without shrinking font or sacrificing top spacing; otherwise continue on further pages with the same top spacing.
- PDF-first, inspect rendered layout, preserve exact names/numbers/sections as verified against attachments.

G.R. Case No. [number/year]

Under Sections [verified provisions]

**State**  
Versus  
**[Every accused/petitioner full name]**

<div style="text-align:right; margin-left:35%">
The humble Surrender Cum Bail Petition filed on behalf of the accused/petitioner(s) [ALL petitioners' names].

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

1. That the petitioners are surrendering voluntarily before this Learned Court today and seek enlargement on bail in the above-noted case.
2. [Precisely grounded paragraph about alleged offence, without admissions or invented details.]
3. [Further verified individual grounds, suitably adapted to the supplied records.]
4. [Previous bail application history, accurately ascertained; do not assume no previous application.]
5. [Undertaking to cooperate with trial, appear when directed, and comply with reasonable bail conditions, if authorized.]

**[Prayer indented toward right]** It is, therefore, humbly prayed that Your Honour may graciously be pleased to enlarge the above-named petitioners on bail on such terms and conditions as this Learned Court may deem fit and proper.

And for this act of kindness, the petitioners, as in duty bound, shall ever pray.

## Essential checks before exporting
1. Verify the court and judge, G.R. and P.S. identifiers, alleged sections, names and petitioner count character by character from current source images.
2. Don't confuse G.R. 182 with 162 or names of the police station.
3. Avoid unsupported factual statements or procedural claims.
4. Use no certificate/signature line by default, and maintain the right-side opening block and Times/Tinos font.
