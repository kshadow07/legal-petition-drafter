# Informatory Petition: Section 33 BNSS heading variant

## User-requested heading (25 October 2026)

Use this wording **only when the user expressly specifies Section 33 BNSS**:

> Informatory Petition under Section 33 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023

Place below the court caption, before `Informatory Petition No. ________ of 20__`.

## Important scope limitation

Section 33 BNSS, 2023 is titled “Public to give information of certain offences” and concerns a statutory duty to report specified offences or intended offences to a Magistrate or police officer. It is **not a general-purpose authority for preventive injunctions or immunity against future complaints**. Verify its applicability to the alleged facts before describing the petition as maintainable under this provision. For matters solely alleging threats of false complaints, retain the client's exact requested title in a review draft but flag the section for advocate review; do not falsely assert the statute supports the relief requested.

## Formatting defaults

Court caption once; A4; English Times New Roman (Tinos permitted fallback), Hindi Noto Sans Devanagari; 85mm top whitespace on **every** page per current AGENTS.md; prayer paragraph indented as a block toward the right, no separate Prayer heading, closing sentence left-aligned. Preserve original party names, allegations, dates and annexures. This is a reusable **heading and layout variant only**, not a factual template.
