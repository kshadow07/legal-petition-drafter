# Simple affidavit

Default format for any simple notarised affidavit of numbered statements by one deponent (for example transfer of an electric connection, name or address declaration, no-dues or no-objection statement). The user approved this format on 16 October 2026. Approval covers the format only; every name, number and date must come from the current matter's papers.

## Layout rules
- A4 portrait, single page when content fits, otherwise continue with the same top gap.
- English Times New Roman 12 pt (Liberation Serif / Tinos fallback); approved render used 11.5 pt with 17 pt leading to fit one page. Justified body, black type, no border.
- **Four-to-five-finger blank gap at the top (approx. 78 mm)** so the stamp paper stamps and notary seal are not covered. Overrides the repository's general 85 mm default for this template; follow any gap the user states.
- Title "AFFIDAVIT" centred, bold, underlined.
- Numbered paragraphs with a first-line indent; closing line "Hence this Affidavit." indented the same way.
- Lower block: left "Identified by" with the identifying advocate's name and "Advocate, Jamshedpur"; right "VERIFICATION" paragraph; deponent signature line at the right (bold "DEPONENT" with name below); then "Date" and "Place" on one line; then notary name, "Notary, Jamshedpur" and registration number as plain lines. No boxed attestation.
- Leave signature and seal areas blank. Never copy scanned signatures, stamps, notary seals or stamp-paper serial numbers into the reusable document.
- Small 9 pt footer: "Affidavit of {{deponent_name}} | {{subject_line}}".

## Editable skeleton (replace every {{field}})

**AFFIDAVIT**

I, **{{deponent_name}}**, {{relationship_line, e.g. W/o Late ...}}, by faith {{faith}}, resident of {{full_address_with_PO_PS_town_district_state}}, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare as follows:-

1. {{statement_1}}
2. {{statement_2}}
3. {{statement_3, add or remove numbered statements as the matter needs}}

Hence this Affidavit.

Identified by {{identifying_advocate}}, Advocate, {{place}}  |  **VERIFICATION:** Verified at {{place}} on this {{day}} day of {{month}}, {{year}} that the statements made above are true to the best of my knowledge, belief and information, and nothing material has been concealed therefrom.

**DEPONENT** ({{deponent_name}})

Date: {{date}} | Place: {{place}}

{{notary_name}}, Notary, {{place}}, Reg. No. {{notary_reg_no}}

## Worked example (statement wording only, no client facts)
Transfer of electric connection after the consumer's death:
1. That I am the legally married {{wife_or_husband}} of {{deceased_name}}, deceased, who died on {{date_of_death}} (xerox copy of the death certificate is attached herewith).
2. That during his/her lifetime, an electric connection has been issued in his/her name vide Consumer No. {{consumer_no}}, Account No. {{account_no}}.
3. That after the death of my aforesaid {{relation}}, the electric connection is to be transferred in my name, being his/her legal {{relation}}, and I am ready to bear the cost of the same.
4. That there are no dues pending against the said connection in his/her name; if any are found, I shall be liable to pay the same to {{utility_name, e.g. J.B.V.N.L., Jamshedpur}}.

## Drafting notes
- Keep the source's clause order and substance; fix grammar only.
- Read handwritten insertions on the source and fold them into the clean text; flag any unclear one.
- Check names, relationships, numbers and dates against the source line by line before rendering.
