# Family partition deed

Read `../../AGENTS.md` first, then `../intake-and-ocr/SKILL.md` if handwriting/scanned records are supplied. Use `../court-pdf-generator/SKILL.md` for PDF export. This is structural drafting guidance, **not a client-specific or advocate-approved template**.

## Source integrity
- Read all current-matter pages, noting cancelled words and handwritten corrections. Distinguish principal property, each allotment, recitals and execution clauses.
- Confirm exact names, parentage, party relationship, addresses, date/place of deed, ownership/source of title where supplied, mouza, thana/khata/plot/halka, total square footage, shares and each boundary. Do not invent a title deed or registration/mutation history.
- Check area totals (Schedule B + Schedule C = Schedule A); arithmetic consistency does not itself resolve a disputed physical boundary. Flag inconsistent name/boundary readings and ask focused questions. Use explicitly confirmed client corrections over ambiguous handwriting.
- An uploaded reference deed may inform typography/structure only. **Do not copy its parties, family history, land details, date, witness identities or title history** into the current matter.

## Style
- English **Times New Roman 12 pt**, or **Tinos** if Times New Roman unavailable. Courier/typewriter only by explicit request.
- A4 portrait; restrained bold and underlined headings, traditional legal prose, readable line spacing and sensible paragraph breaks.
- Centre and underline `FAMILY PARTITION DEED`. Introduce First Party with its verified description, then put a centred, spaced, optionally underlined `A N D`, followed by Second Party. Keep party designations consistent.
- Place recitals beginning `WHEREAS` before `NOW THIS FAMILY PARTITION DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS`. Improve grammar but keep the manuscript's factual meaning and original clauses. Do not add new factual recitals.
- Set out allocation, separate possession/enjoyment for each party and heirs/successors, reciprocal non-interference and no claim over the other's allotted share, mutation/rent receipt clause where supported, and binding effect. Avoid categorical guarantees beyond the facts or mandatory law.
- After the operative clauses put **SCHEDULE 'A' (PRINCIPAL PROPERTY)**, **SCHEDULE 'B' (FIRST PARTY'S SHARE)**, and **SCHEDULE 'C' (SECOND PARTY'S SHARE)** with clearly laid-out north/south/east/west boundaries and exact areas. The share descriptions should track the confirmed schedule data. Include a copies/record note only when requested or supported.
- **Execution belongs at the END of the deed, after Schedule C**, not amid the recitals. Put the execution statement and date/place (use blanks if not verified), then signature blocks for First Party and Second Party, followed by `WITNESSES` with numbered blank name/address/signature lines. The witnesses sign the instrument and should not be confused with people named in factual recitals. Leave their details blank unless supplied. Do not copy signatures or names from sample photos.
- Do not automatically apply petition-specific four-finger/three-finger blank space to deeds; match the supplied instrument reference. A first-page signing gap applies only on direct request.

## Final checks
- Confirm all name spellings and land dimensions against current matter's confirmed facts. For example, do not change a southern boundary just because it seems likely to match the principal property's edge without client confirmation.
- Check that the headings, centred `A N D`, paragraph flow, schedules and final witness blocks match the chosen reference's **style**.
- Provide PDF directly when requested; preserve editable source for revisions. Legal validity, stamp duty, registration and title require case-specific review by the advocate, not assumptions based on a sample.
