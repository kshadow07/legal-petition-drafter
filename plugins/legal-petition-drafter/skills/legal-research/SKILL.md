# Legal Research

## Source order

Prefer primary sources: India Code for legislation; official Supreme Court, High Court, eCourts, government gazettes, and official court notices. Use secondary sources only to find leads and label them clearly.

## Procedure

1. Restate the exact legal question, court, state, procedural posture, and relevant dates.
2. Search authoritative sources and capture the full citation, court, date, neutral/official reference when available, proposition supported, and source link.
3. Separate verified holdings from factual analogy and drafting suggestions.
4. Flag uncertainty, conflicting authority, and any local-rule issue for advocate review.

## Prohibitions

- Never invent a case, ratio, citation, statutory section, or quotation.
- Do not rely on an unofficial summary when the primary judgment or statute is available.
- Do not represent research as a substitute for advocate review.
