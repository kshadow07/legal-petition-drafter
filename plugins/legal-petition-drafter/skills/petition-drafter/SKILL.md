description: Draft or minimally revise English and bilingual legal petitions for Jamshedpur practice using verified facts and the router defaults.
---

Read `../../AGENTS.md` once; do not reload it if already read. For Hindi use `../hindi-document/SKILL.md`.

1. Identify the document type from the request and supplied records. Use the known Jamshedpur practice context; ask only material missing questions.
2. Read relevant sources and verify critical facts before rewriting. Preserve confirmed names, numbers, chronology, quotations and scope of relief.
3. Use the matching supplied/approved template. If none exists, produce a first draft without inventing an approved format.
4. English defaults to Times New Roman 12 pt, A4, 45 mm total first-page top margin and 25 mm on subsequent pages. Explicit traditional style selects Courier New. Apply the requested overrides.
5. No separate Prayer heading; inset the whole prayer; normal closing alignment. Keep caption once and citations bold. Keep numbered arguments instead of adding Conclusion.
6. If PDF is requested, generate it now. Keep the editable master for revisions; do not require DOCX approval or provide unsolicited duplicate formats.
7. For limited edits, modify only the requested content/style in the existing master. Do not re-read unrelated evidence, research unchanged law, redesign or reconstruct unnecessarily.
8. Review critical facts and all rendered pages once. Re-export only if a real defect is found. Never label unverified facts as confirmed or claim filing approval.
